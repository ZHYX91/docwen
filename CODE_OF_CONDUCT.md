# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Discuss the work rather than the person, respect privacy, and do
not publish another person's documents, paths, credentials, or personal information. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect users or
the project. Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile rather than placing
sensitive details in a public issue.
